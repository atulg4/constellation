# Mission 013 - Enterprise Foundation

## Summary

Layer enterprise identity, policy, relay, observability, and compliance primitives without compromising the open core.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Enterprise identity bindings are optional.
- [ ] Managed relays/gateways preserve end-to-end content security unless explicit policy says otherwise.
- [ ] Audit modes are namespace/policy scoped.

## Required Tests

- Enterprise identity binding tests.
- Policy enforcement tests.
- Audit logging privacy review.

## Non-Goals

- Mandatory enterprise CA.
- Closed protocol fork.

## Dependencies

Missions 002, 008, and 010.
