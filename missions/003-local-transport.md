# Mission 003 - Local Transport

## Summary

Harden local transport adapters and capability reporting across target platforms.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Transport adapter trait is stable.
- [ ] At least two local adapters expose capability metadata.
- [ ] Permission and foreground requirements are documented and handled.

## Required Tests

- Adapter contract tests.
- Discovery/connect/send/receive failure tests.
- Platform smoke tests.

## Non-Goals

- Background always-on relaying.
- Satellite or public Internet transport.

## Dependencies

Mission 001.
